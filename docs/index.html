Built for how the business actually runs

Every person sees only what their role allows. Each station, farm and property keeps its own books.

Fuel stations

Point of sale by litres or amount, shifts, pump readings, tank dips, deliveries and price changes with approval.

Dairy farm

Herd register, milk per cow per session, treatments with withdrawal holds, breeding, feed stock and milk sales.

Rental properties

Units, tenants, leases, rent and utility billing, deposits, payments to verify and maintenance requests.

Finance

A separate ledger per location, bank and M-PESA statement matching, bills, period locks and consolidated reports.

Works offline

Records are saved on the phone, encrypted, and sent when there is a connection. Nothing is lost or counted twice.

Secure by default

Personal sign-in for everyone, auto-lock, approved phones only if you choose, an activity log nobody can edit and encrypted backups.

Install in three steps

  1. Download the APK on the phone using the button above.
  2. Open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser; turn on Allow from this source and go back.
  3. Install and open Chalkman. Staff sign in with the username and password their administrator gave them. A new business taps Start with a licence key and enters the key from RG DEVs; a short guided setup follows.

Need a licence key for your business? Talk to RG DEVs on WhatsApp. Your first sign-in needs a connection. After that the app works offline within the limits your Owner sets.

Updates arrive by themselves

When a new version is published, Chalkman shows Update available the next time it opens (or from More → Check for updates). It downloads, checks the file and hands it to Android to install. Records waiting to sync stay on the phone through the update.
